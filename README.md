# Body Worn Camera Deployment & Evidence Management: A Practical Guide for Security Teams | Gvtel Communication System

## About This Guide

The **Body Worn Camera Deployment & Evidence Management: A Practical Guide for Security Teams** by **Gvtel Communication System** is a practical resource for organisations planning to use body worn cameras in security operations, field activities and incident documentation.

This guide explains how to plan a body worn camera deployment, assign devices to staff, establish recording procedures and manage recorded footage. It also highlights the importance of device accountability, shift handovers, organised evidence storage and controlled access to video records.

The aim is to help security teams establish a consistent process for using body worn cameras and managing recorded evidence throughout its lifecycle.

## Topics Covered

- Planning a body worn camera deployment
- Assigning devices and maintaining accountability
- Device allocation and shift handover procedures
- Establishing standard recording procedures
- Managing recorded footage and incident records
- Organising video evidence for review and reference
- Understanding available body worn camera models
- Practical deployment workflow
- Body worn camera deployment checklist
- Frequently Asked Questions
